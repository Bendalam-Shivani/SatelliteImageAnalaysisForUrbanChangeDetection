# SatelliteImageAnalysisForUrbanChangeDetection

Monitoring urban sprawl is a critical step for urban planners and environmentalists aiming to
manage sustainable regional development. Traditionally, analyzing land-use changes over vast
areas like the Godavari region can be a data-intensive and computationally difficult experience.
The project describes an automated, machine-learning-based application intended to assist
researchers and policymakers in detecting and predicting urban change. The system utilizes
multi-temporal Landsat 8 satellite imagery to compute specialized spectral indices, such as
NDVI, MNDWI, NDBI, and BAEI, providing a high-fidelity representation of surface
characteristics. Beyond simple detection, the application offers analytical features to track
spatiotemporal progress and analyze the rate of land transformation. A key feature of the
proposed system is its future prediction capability, which leverages historical growth trends to
forecast urban expansion for upcoming years. The inclusion of intelligent capabilities
simplifies the complex field of remote sensing, making advanced satellite analysis accessible
for effective environmental oversight and sustainable planning.
